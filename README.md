# Expense Tracking App – Main Features

### 1. Expense Tracking 💰

* Add and manage daily expenses.
* Save details such as amount, description, category, and date.
* Example: Food – ₹200, Travel – ₹500, Shopping – ₹1,000.

### 2. Financial Dashboard 📊

View all important financial information in one dashboard:

* Total Spending
* Today's Spending
* Current Month's Spending
* Total Transactions

### 3. Monthly Budget 💵

* Set a budget for each month.
* Example: Monthly Budget – ₹30,000.
* Track how much money has been spent and how much is remaining.

### 4. Due & Payment Tracker 🔔

* Track money that you need to pay to others.
* Track money that you are supposed to receive from others.
* Manage pending payments easily.

### 5. EMI Tracker 🏦

Save and manage EMI or purchase details, including:

* EMI/Purchase Name
* Monthly EMI Amount
* Due Date
* Payment Reminder

### 6. Analytics & Reports 📈

* Identify which expense categories are consuming the most money.
* Analyze spending using different date ranges.
* View total spending, total transactions, and average transaction amount.

### 7. AI Spending Insights 🤖

* Compare the current month's spending with the previous month.
* Analyze daily spending patterns.
* Provide an estimated projection of how much you may spend by the end of the month.

### 8. CSV Export 📁

* Export expense data as a CSV file.
* Useful for analyzing data in Excel or keeping records for future reference.

### 9. Print Bills 🖨️

* Print expense and bill information whenever required.

### 10. Login & Google Sign-In 🔐

* Log in using your account credentials.
* Sign in easily using Google.
* Reset your password if you forget it.

### 11. Dark Mode 🌙

* Switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode according to your preference.

---

## In Simple Terms

This app combines:

**Expense Tracker + Budget Planner + EMI Tracker + Due Payment Manager + Financial Dashboard + Spending Analytics + AI Spending Insights**

In other words, the app allows you to **manage your finances in one place**. You can track where your monthly income is being spent, how much money is remaining, which categories have the highest expenses, your pending payments and EMIs, and even get AI-powered insights about your future spending.
